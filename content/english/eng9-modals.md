# Modal Verbs

**Modal verbs** are helping verbs that show ability, possibility, permission, obligation, advice or requests. The main modals are *can, could, may, might, must, shall, should, will, would*. Expressions like *have to, ought to* and *be able to* work in a similar way.

## Grammar rules

- A modal is followed by the **base form** of the verb: *She can **swim*** (not *can swims* or *can to swim*).
- Modals never add -s: *He must go* (not *He musts go*).
- Negatives add *not*: *cannot (can't), should not (shouldn't), must not (mustn't)*.
- Questions put the modal first: *Can you help me? Should I call her?*

## Meanings

- **Ability**: *can* (present), *could* (past), *be able to* (any tense). *I can speak three languages. When I was five, I could already read. She will be able to drive next year.*
- **Permission**: *can* (informal), *may* (formal), *could* (polite). *Can I borrow your pen? May I leave the room?*
- **Requests**: *can, could, will, would*. *Could you open the window? Would you help me carry this?* (*Could* and *would* are more polite.)
- **Possibility**: *may, might, could* (less certain). *It may rain later. He might be at the canteen.*
- **Strong certainty / deduction**: *must* (sure it is true), *can't* (sure it isn't). *The lights are off — they must be asleep. That can't be Ana; she's in Cebu.*
- **Obligation / necessity**: *must* (rule or strong personal feeling), *have to* (outside rule). *Students must wear their IDs. I have to finish this by Friday.*
- **No obligation vs. prohibition**: *don't have to* = not necessary; *must not* = not allowed. *You don't have to come* (it's optional). *You must not cheat* (it's forbidden).
- **Advice**: *should, ought to*. *You should drink more water. You ought to apologize.*
- **Offers and suggestions**: *shall* (mostly with *I/we*), *can*. *Shall we start? I can carry that for you.*

## Modals about the past

Modal + *have* + past participle talks about the past:
- *You **should have** told me.* (It was a good idea, but you didn't.)
- *She **must have** forgotten.* (I'm almost sure she forgot.)
- *They **might have** missed the bus.* (Possibly.)

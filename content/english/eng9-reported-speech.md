# Direct and Reported Speech

**Direct speech** gives a speaker's exact words inside quotation marks. **Reported (indirect) speech** tells what someone said without quoting them exactly.

- Direct: *Maria said, "I am tired."*
- Reported: *Maria said (that) she was tired.*

## Punctuating direct speech

- Put the exact words in quotation marks and start them with a capital letter.
- Use a comma between the reporting verb and the quote: *He said, "Let's go."* or *"Let's go," he said.*
- Punctuation that belongs to the quote goes inside the quotation marks: *"Are you ready?" she asked.*

## Changing direct to reported speech

When the reporting verb is in the past (*said, told, asked*), three things usually change.

**1. Tense moves one step back**
- simple present → simple past: *"I **like** it."* → *She said she **liked** it.*
- present progressive → past progressive: *"I **am working**."* → *He said he **was working**.*
- simple past / present perfect → past perfect: *"I **saw** it." / "I **have seen** it."* → *She said she **had seen** it.*
- will → would, can → could, may → might, must → had to.

If the statement is still true or a general fact, the tense can stay: *He said that the earth **goes** around the sun.*

**2. Pronouns change to fit the new speaker**
- *"I will help **you**," Ben told me.* → *Ben told me **he** would help **me**.*

**3. Time and place words change**
- now → then; today → that day; tomorrow → the next day / the following day; yesterday → the day before / the previous day; here → there; this → that; next week → the following week; ago → before.

## Questions, commands and requests

- **Yes/no questions** use *if* or *whether*, with statement word order: *"Are you coming?"* → *She asked **if I was** coming.*
- **Wh- questions** keep the question word, with statement word order: *"Where do you live?"* → *He asked **where I lived**.*
- **Commands and requests** use *told/asked* + object + *to* + base verb: *"Close the door."* → *She told me **to close** the door. "Please don't shout."* → *He asked us **not to shout**.*

## Reporting verbs

*Said* is not the only choice. Others show how something was said: *explained, replied, admitted, suggested, promised, warned, complained, insisted*. Use *told* with a person: *She told **me**…* (not *She said me…*).

# Elements of a Story

Short stories, folktales, myths and legends share the same basic parts. Knowing them helps you understand and discuss any story, including Philippine folktales such as the legend of the pineapple.

## Setting and characters

- **Setting** is where and when the story happens: a fishing village in Samar, a city during a typhoon, a kingdom long ago. The setting can shape the mood and the problem.
- **Characters** are the people or animals in the story.
  - The **protagonist** is the main character.
  - The **antagonist** opposes the protagonist (a person, a group, nature or even the character's own fear).
  - A **round** character is complex and changes; a **flat** character has one or two traits and doesn't change.
- **Characterization** is how the author shows a character: through what the character says, does and thinks, and what others say about them.

## Plot

The **plot** is the sequence of events.
1. **Exposition**: introduces the characters, setting and situation.
2. **Rising action**: problems and events build tension.
3. **Climax**: the turning point, the most exciting or important moment.
4. **Falling action**: events after the climax that lead to the end.
5. **Resolution (denouement)**: the problem is solved or the story ends.

## Conflict

**Conflict** is the struggle that drives the plot.
- **Person vs. person**: two characters are against each other.
- **Person vs. nature**: a character struggles with a storm, flood or wild animal.
- **Person vs. society**: a character goes against rules, traditions or a group.
- **Person vs. self**: a character struggles with a fear, a choice or guilt (internal conflict).

## Point of view and theme

- **Point of view** is who tells the story. **First person** uses *I* and is told by a character. **Third person** uses *he, she, they* and is told by an outside narrator.
- **Theme** is the message or lesson about life, such as "hard work brings success" or "greed leads to loss." A theme is usually not stated directly; you infer it from what the characters learn.
